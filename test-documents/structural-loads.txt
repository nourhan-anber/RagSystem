KESTREL COUNTY BUILDING CODE (KCBC)
Part 3 - Structural Design Loads
Revision 4.2, adopted 11 February 2029

3.1 SCOPE

This Part sets out the minimum design loads for buildings within Kestrel County.
Where a structure falls under Part 12 (Heritage Structures), the provisions of
Part 12 take precedence over this Part.

3.2 MINIMUM UNIFORMLY DISTRIBUTED LIVE LOADS

Clause 3.2.1. Floors shall be designed for not less than the following uniformly
distributed live loads:

  Residential dwelling units          1.9 kN/m2
  Office areas above the first floor  2.7 kN/m2
  Office areas at grade               4.1 kN/m2
  Assembly areas with fixed seating   3.6 kN/m2
  Assembly areas without seating      4.8 kN/m2
  Light storage                       6.2 kN/m2
  Heavy storage                       12.0 kN/m2
  Stair treads and landings           4.8 kN/m2
  Vehicle parking, passenger only     2.4 kN/m2

Clause 3.2.2. Live loads on office floors may be reduced by 0.5 percent per square
metre of tributary area, to a maximum reduction of 40 percent. No reduction is
permitted for assembly areas or storage areas.

3.3 SNOW LOADS

Clause 3.3.1. The basic ground snow load for sites below 400 metres elevation is
1.4 kN/m2. For sites at or above 400 metres, the basic ground snow load is
2.6 kN/m2.

Clause 3.3.2. Roofs with a slope exceeding 30 degrees may apply a slope reduction
factor of 0.75. Roofs with a slope exceeding 60 degrees need not be designed for
snow accumulation, except at valleys and parapets.

Clause 3.3.3. Drift loading shall be considered wherever a roof abuts a wall
taller than 1.2 metres.

3.4 WIND LOADS

Clause 3.4.1. The basic wind speed for Kestrel County is 42 metres per second,
measured as a three-second gust at 10 metres above grade in open terrain.

Clause 3.4.2. Exposure categories are assigned as follows. Exposure B applies to
urban and suburban areas with numerous closely spaced obstructions. Exposure C
applies to open terrain with scattered obstructions under 9 metres in height.
Exposure D applies to flat unobstructed areas within 1.5 kilometres of a large
body of water.

Clause 3.4.3. Buildings taller than 60 metres, or with a height to least width
ratio greater than 5, shall be assessed by wind tunnel testing.

3.5 SEISMIC DESIGN

Clause 3.5.1. Kestrel County is assigned Seismic Design Category C. The importance
factor for standard occupancy buildings is 1.0, for essential facilities 1.5, and
for hazardous material facilities 1.25.

Clause 3.5.2. Structures exceeding 20 metres in height shall use dynamic analysis
rather than the equivalent static force method.

3.6 DEFLECTION LIMITS

Clause 3.6.1. Deflection under live load alone shall not exceed span divided by
360. Deflection under combined dead and live load shall not exceed span divided
by 240.

Clause 3.6.2. For members supporting brittle finishes, deflection under live load
shall not exceed span divided by 480.

Clause 3.6.3. Lateral drift between adjacent storeys shall not exceed 0.4 percent
of the storey height under service level wind loading.
