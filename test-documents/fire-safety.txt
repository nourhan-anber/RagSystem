KESTREL COUNTY BUILDING CODE (KCBC)
Part 5 - Fire Protection and Means of Egress
Revision 4.2, adopted 11 February 2029

5.1 SCOPE

This Part governs fire resistance construction, detection and suppression systems,
and means of egress. Where this Part conflicts with Part 7 (Accessibility), the
more restrictive requirement applies, except that accessible route widths under
Part 7 shall never be reduced.

5.2 OCCUPANT LOAD

Clause 5.2.1. Occupant load shall be calculated using the following floor area
allowances per person:

  Assembly, standing space         0.6 m2 per person
  Assembly, tables and chairs      1.4 m2 per person
  Business and office              9.3 m2 per person
  Educational classrooms           1.9 m2 per person
  Mercantile, ground floor         5.6 m2 per person
  Storage and warehouse           46.0 m2 per person
  Residential dwelling            18.6 m2 per person

Clause 5.2.2. Where a space serves more than one function, the occupant load shall
be the sum of the loads for each function calculated separately.

5.3 TRAVEL DISTANCE

Clause 5.3.1. The maximum travel distance from any point in a storey to the
nearest exit shall not exceed 45 metres in a building protected throughout by an
automatic sprinkler system, or 30 metres in a building without such protection.

Clause 5.3.2. In storage occupancies the maximum travel distance is 60 metres
where sprinklered and 38 metres where unsprinklered.

Clause 5.3.3. Dead end corridors shall not exceed 6 metres in length.

5.4 EXIT CAPACITY AND WIDTH

Clause 5.4.1. Egress capacity shall be provided at 5.1 millimetres of width per
occupant for stairways, and 3.8 millimetres per occupant for level egress
components including corridors and doorways.

Clause 5.4.2. The minimum clear width of an exit corridor is 1,120 millimetres.
The minimum clear width of an exit stairway is 1,120 millimetres.

Clause 5.4.3. Fire door assemblies in an exit enclosure shall have a minimum leaf
width of 900 millimetres.

Clause 5.4.4. Every storey with an occupant load exceeding 49 persons shall have
not fewer than two exits. Every storey with an occupant load exceeding 500 persons
shall have not fewer than three exits.

5.5 FIRE RESISTANCE RATINGS

Clause 5.5.1. Exit stair enclosures shall have a fire resistance rating of not
less than 2 hours where the enclosure connects four or more storeys, and not less
than 1 hour where it connects fewer than four storeys.

Clause 5.5.2. Fire doors within a 2 hour rated enclosure shall carry a 90 minute
rating. Fire doors within a 1 hour rated enclosure shall carry a 60 minute rating.

Clause 5.5.3. Shaft enclosures for elevators shall have a fire resistance rating
of not less than 2 hours.

Clause 5.5.4. Separation between dwelling units shall have a fire resistance
rating of not less than 1 hour, increased to 90 minutes where the building exceeds
six storeys.

5.6 AUTOMATIC SPRINKLER SYSTEMS

Clause 5.6.1. An automatic sprinkler system is required throughout any building
that exceeds 1,200 square metres in aggregate floor area, or that exceeds three
storeys in height, or that contains an assembly occupancy with an occupant load
exceeding 300 persons.

Clause 5.6.2. Sprinkler water supply shall be sized for a duration of not less
than 60 minutes for light hazard occupancies and 90 minutes for ordinary hazard
occupancies.

5.7 FIRE ALARM AND DETECTION

Clause 5.7.1. A manual fire alarm system is required in buildings exceeding two
storeys or with an occupant load exceeding 100 persons.

Clause 5.7.2. Smoke detection is required in every corridor serving sleeping
rooms, at intervals not exceeding 9 metres.
