KESTREL COUNTY BUILDING CODE (KCBC)
Part 7 - Accessibility
Revision 4.2, adopted 11 February 2029

7.1 SCOPE

This Part applies to all new construction and to alterations affecting more than
50 percent of the floor area of an existing storey. Buildings under Part 12
(Heritage Structures) may apply to the Building Official for partial relief.

7.2 ACCESSIBLE ROUTES

Clause 7.2.1. An accessible route shall have a clear width of not less than
1,200 millimetres, except that it may narrow to 900 millimetres for a length not
exceeding 600 millimetres at a single point.

Clause 7.2.2. Where an accessible route requires a 180 degree turn around an
obstruction, the clear width shall be not less than 1,500 millimetres.

Clause 7.2.3. A turning space shall be provided at intervals not exceeding
60 metres along an accessible route. A turning space is a circle of not less than
1,500 millimetres diameter, or a T-shaped space within a 1,500 millimetre square.

7.3 DOORS AND DOORWAYS

Clause 7.3.1. Doorways on an accessible route shall provide a clear opening width
of not less than 850 millimetres, measured with the door open 90 degrees.

Clause 7.3.2. The maximum force required to open an interior door shall not
exceed 22 newtons. For exterior doors the maximum is 38 newtons.

Clause 7.3.3. Door hardware shall be operable with one hand without tight
grasping, pinching, or twisting of the wrist, and shall be mounted between
900 and 1,200 millimetres above the finished floor.

Clause 7.3.4. Where a door opens onto an accessible route, manoeuvring clearance
of not less than 450 millimetres shall be provided on the latch side.

7.4 RAMPS

Clause 7.4.1. The running slope of a ramp shall not exceed 1 in 12. The cross
slope shall not exceed 1 in 50.

Clause 7.4.2. The maximum rise of a single ramp run is 760 millimetres. A level
landing not less than 1,500 millimetres long shall be provided at the top and
bottom of each run.

Clause 7.4.3. Ramps rising more than 150 millimetres shall have handrails on both
sides, mounted between 865 and 965 millimetres above the ramp surface.

Clause 7.4.4. Edge protection of not less than 100 millimetres in height is
required along both sides of a ramp run.

7.5 SANITARY FACILITIES

Clause 7.5.1. Grab bars shall be mounted between 750 and 850 millimetres above
the finished floor.

Clause 7.5.2. The clear floor space at an accessible water closet shall be not
less than 1,500 millimetres by 1,500 millimetres.

Clause 7.5.3. The rim of an accessible lavatory shall be not more than
865 millimetres above the finished floor, with knee clearance of not less than
685 millimetres.

7.6 PARKING

Clause 7.6.1. Accessible parking spaces shall be provided at the rate of one
space for every 25 parking spaces for the first 100 spaces, then one space for
every 50 spaces thereafter.

Clause 7.6.2. An accessible parking space shall be not less than 2,700
millimetres wide with an adjacent access aisle not less than 1,500 millimetres
wide. Van accessible spaces require an access aisle of not less than 2,400
millimetres.

Clause 7.6.3. Accessible parking shall be located on the shortest accessible
route to the accessible entrance, and in no case more than 45 metres from it.

7.7 SIGNAGE AND ALARMS

Clause 7.7.1. Tactile characters on signage shall be raised not less than
0.8 millimetres and shall be between 16 and 50 millimetres in height.

Clause 7.7.2. Visual alarm appliances shall be provided in every public corridor,
toilet room, and assembly space, synchronised at a flash rate of 1 hertz.
